-Delivered AI governance briefings to 100+ municipal and county stakeholders across civic assemblies, and drove adoption of responsible AI frameworks at the county level.
-Organized AI policy panels and workshops that connected and coordinated legislative teams, civil rights leaders, researchers, and CTOs to discuss critical policy needs and adoption successes.
-Consulted on AI governance roadmaps and conducted responsible AI reviews for local and state government and community organizations, translating AI risk into practice and policy. 

### Tenured Associate Professor (Research Program Director)  |  University of Calgary	Aug 2016 – Aug 2025
-Administered a $2M+ portfolio of 12+ research and technology projects, supervising teams of up to 20 analysts and specialists. Delegated, coordinated, and synthesized team work and outputs.
-Led the City of Calgary's open data modernization initiative, conducting client interviews that shaped platform and policy decisions.
-Co-authored Alberta's Non-profit Data Strategy, identifying gaps and best practices to steer provincial data sharing policy. Drew on 12 interviews with professionals and policymakers.

-Directed "AI for Global Public Health" project (team of 9) in partnership with Seattle non-profit, delivering 4 AI solutions to improve healthcare worker route planning, and better assess program outcomes (2021).
